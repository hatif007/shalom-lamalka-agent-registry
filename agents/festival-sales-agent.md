# Festival Sales Agent

## Status
DEFERRED — reliable festival booth sales and cost data are not currently available.

## Purpose when activated
Analyze booth revenue, product mix, costs, and festival-level profitability once reliable source data is provided.

## Required inputs
At minimum: transaction date, festival/event identifier, SKU/product, quantity, gross sales, discounts/refunds, payment fees, direct costs, and event-related costs. Avoid customer personal data unless necessary.

## Constraints
Do not infer festival sales from Wix online orders. Do not activate or claim live analysis until the source data is available and reviewed. READ ONLY by default.
