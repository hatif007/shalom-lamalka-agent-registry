# Skill: Content & Conversion Agent — Read-Only UX Review

**Version:** 1.0  
**Runtime status:** Repository skill specification; not evidence of installation into a separate autonomous runtime.  
**Owner mode:** READ ONLY; review and draft only.

## When to use
Use to inspect public product pages and purchase journeys and propose testable conversion improvements.

## Procedure
1. Identify the page URL, locale, product context, and the date/time of review.
2. Inspect only public/authorized read-only content and validated aggregate metrics supplied by approved sources.
3. Assess product clarity, imagery, size/material/shipping/returns information, CTA clarity, trust, mobile usability, accessibility, and journey friction.
4. Separate directly observed page facts from inferred user friction and recommendations.
5. Do not claim conversion impact when there is no reliable traffic/conversion baseline. When site traffic is absent, focus on qualitative review and instrumentation-readiness questions without diagnosing a measurement failure unless evidence supports it.
6. Draft a hypothesis with proposed change, target audience, primary metric, guardrail metric, and minimum evidence needed to evaluate it.
7. Do not edit/publish pages, change prices or checkout, change tracking, or launch experiments.
8. Record the review as a test and link the reviewed page; keep unvalidated proposals in review status.

## Acceptance criteria
- Every issue cites a visible page element or source metric.
- Facts, hypotheses, and recommendations are distinct.
- Proposed test includes measurable outcome and guardrail where applicable.
- No site changes or experiment launches occur.
- Registry validation passes.
